# The World Today — Thursday, 26 November 2026

A one-page front-page image of the day's news: one hero story and nine
more stories from around the world, each short enough to read at a
glance. Every story below was cross-checked against at least two
independent sources before it was drawn.

## Hero story

**Houthis claim strike on Saudi Arabia's largest oil refinery.**
Yemen's Houthis said they struck the Ras Tanura refinery on Saudi
Arabia's Gulf coast, and satellite images reviewed by Reuters showed a
large black smoke plume over the site. Saudi Arabia said its air
defences shot down four drones and did not confirm a direct hit. Brent
crude jumped past $93 a barrel after the claim.
Sources: Reuters; CGTN America; Middle East Eye; Firstpost (satellite imagery).

## The other nine stories

1. **Cairo — Fuel tanker fire kills at least 30.** A tanker carrying
   petroleum products overturned on the Ring Road in Giza and caught
   fire, killing at least 30 people and injuring 25; the toll was
   described as preliminary. Sources: Associated Press; Reuters; CNN.
2. **Space — First Pakistani in orbit reaches Tiangong.** China's
   Shenzhou-24 launched on 25 November and docked with the Tiangong
   space station; payload specialist Muhammad Zeeshan Ali became the
   first Pakistani in orbit and the first foreign visitor to Tiangong.
   Sources: Associated Press; Nature; Reuters.
3. **Bangladesh — Garment wages up 36% after protests.** The wage
   board raised the minimum monthly wage for garment workers to 17,000
   taka (about $136–139) from 12,500 taka, after weeks of protests that
   shut factories. Workers had demanded far more; fresh protests
   followed. Sources: Associated Press; Reuters; Bloomberg.
4. **Korea — Pyongyang expels Swiss envoy.** North Korea ordered
   Switzerland to close its newly opened diplomatic office in Pyongyang
   and told its head, Sylvain Astier, to leave within 48 hours, in
   retaliation for Swiss sanctions on the Songdowon children's camp.
   Sources: Yonhap (via KCNA); Al Jazeera.
5. **Gaza — Israeli strike kills Hamas security chief.** An Israeli
   airstrike on an apartment in Gaza City's Shati refugee camp killed
   Hamas colonel Mohammad Abuelouf, who headed the group's internal
   security service in northern Gaza, along with his wife and two
   daughters — the second senior Hamas security official killed in 24
   hours. Sources: Reuters; Xinhua.
6. **The Gulf — Two tankers hit mines in Hormuz.** Iran's Revolutionary
   Guard navy said two oil tankers struck naval mines and caught fire
   while trying to transit the Strait of Hormuz on a route Iran calls
   unauthorised. The claims could not be independently verified.
   Sources: IRNA (via OilPrice); DefenceMENA.
7. **USA — Six die in plane crash off Mississippi.** A single-engine
   Cessna 210 flying from New Orleans to Fort Myers crashed into the
   Gulf of Mexico near Horn Island; all six aboard died, including two
   boys. The US Coast Guard recovered the bodies from the fuselage.
   Sources: US Coast Guard; Associated Press; USA Today.
8. **Chess — Gukesh–Sindarov: 20-move draw.** Game 2 of the World
   Chess Championship in Geneva ended in a 20-move draw by repetition,
   leaving the match level at 1–1. Sources: ChessBase; Lichess; The
   Guardian.
9. **Cricket — 15-year-old smashes 204 off 53 balls.** India's Vaibhav
   Sooryavanshi hit 204 off 53 balls for India A against the UAE in
   Doha on 24 November, setting List A records for the fastest century
   (28 balls), fastest double century (52 balls) and most sixes in an
   innings (28). Sources: ESPNcricinfo; The Times.

## How to run

`build_image.py` draws `entry.png` (1024×1024 PNG) with Pillow.
`main.py` is standard-library only: it copies `entry.png` to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` (default `/output`).
